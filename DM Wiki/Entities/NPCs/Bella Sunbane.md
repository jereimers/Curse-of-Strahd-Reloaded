# Who they are

Bella Sunbane (also known as Belladonna) is one of [[Morgantha]]'s two daughters and a member of the night hag coven dwelling at [[Old Bonegrinder]]. Together with her mother and sister [[Offalia Wormwiggle|Offalia]], she grinds children's bones into the addictive [[Dream Pastry|dream pastries]] they sell to desperate refugees.

# What the party knows

- One of the three night hags of the [[Old Bonegrinder]] coven
- Her true name, **Belladonna Sunbane**, was discovered written on the coven's contract (inscribed on dried human skin)
- The contract was hidden in a secret compartment within the windmill's millstone

# Where we've seen them

- **[[Session 19 - The Con at Old Bonegrinder]]** (Neyavr 7) - Called downstairs by [[Morgantha]] to hear the business proposal from "Tyrone & Sons Holdings, LLC" ([[Arden Nalero]] and [[Varnish Gothorn]] in disguise). Descended the stairs with her sister [[Offalia Wormwiggle|Offalia]], narrowly missing the invisible [[Fig Fleetfoot]] and [[Choppy Gibbles]] who were pressed against the stairway wall. Joined her mother and sister in enthusiastically discussing the proposal to expand their dream pastry distribution network through Vistani connections. Remained completely unaware that while she was distracted, the party's infiltration team stole the coven's contract and rescued the captive children Fyodor and Myrtle from the third floor.
