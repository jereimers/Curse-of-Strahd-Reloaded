# Overview

The Old Bonegrinder is a squat stone windmill of ill omen, situated along the [[Old Svalich Road]] between [[Tser Falls]] and [[Town of Vallaki|Vallaki]]. Once known as the Durst Mill, the party actually holds a deed to this property—discovered during their harrowing escape from the [[Haunted House|Durst House]]. However, the windmill is currently occupied by [[Morgantha]] and her daughters, who have made it clear they have no intention of vacating.

# What the party knows

- The party possesses a deed to the windmill from the [[Haunted House|Durst Estate]]
- [[Morgantha]] and her daughters currently reside there and refuse to acknowledge the party's claim
- [[Morgantha]] threatened to use the deed as kindling if the party cited it again
- [[Morgantha]] gave each party member a free [[Dream Pastry]], recommending they be enjoyed after a meal with red wine
- [[Varnish Gothorn|Varnish]] had previously consumed one of [[Morgantha]]'s pastries and experienced disturbing visions of amber tombs and cosmic suffering
- The windmill has three floors: ground floor with bone mill and kitchen, second floor with storage, third floor with bedroom
- A copper key was hidden in the millstone's gnarled wooden crossbeam
- The key opened a secret compartment containing the hags' coven contract (written on dried human skin), cheap jewelry, and a *charm of mass heroism*
- The three hags are [[Morgantha]] (true name: **Morgantha Stormreaver**), [[Bella Sunbane]] (true name: **Belladonna Sunbane**), and [[Offalia Wormwiggle]]
- The hags were holding two Barovian children captive: Fyodor and Myrtle (now rescued)

<!-- DM-Only Start -->
The Old Bonegrinder is the lair of a coven of night hags who use it to produce [[Dream Pastry|Dream Pastries]]—enchanted confections made from the life force of children. Morgantha and her daughters are far more dangerous than they appear.

# Visits

- [[Session 08 - Hairy Bill, Bairy Hill, and a Coin for the Ladies Three#The Old Bonegrinder and Morgantha|Session 08]] - Encountered [[Morgantha]] on the path to the windmill; received free dream pastries
- **[[Session 15 - Killing Izek]]** (Neyavr 5, Morning) - [[Victor Vallakovich]] showed the party a blueprint of the Old Bonegrinder from his father's library. The architectural drawing revealed a hidden compartment within the millstone structure where the hags' coven contract might be stored. This intelligence proved crucial for planning their eventual confrontation with the night hag coven and retrieving [[Morgantha]]'s [[Heartstone|heartstone]] needed for the Ethereal Plane ritual.
- **[[Session 19 - The Con at Old Bonegrinder]]** (Neyavr 7) - Executed an elaborate con operation to infiltrate the windmill and rescue the captive children. [[Lady Fiona Wachter]] assembled a rescue team including [[Victor Vallakovich]], [[Ireena Kolyana]], and her four cultists (Boris, Andrej, Miruna, and Ruxandra). At the windmill, the party split into two teams: [[Victor Vallakovich]] cast *greater invisibility* on [[Fig Fleetfoot]] and [[Choppy Gibbles]] to create an invisible infiltration team, while [[Varnish Gothorn]] and [[Arden Nalero]] posed as businessmen "Emric Cantemir" and "Radu Tyminski" of "Tyrone & Sons Holdings, LLC" to distract the hags with a lucrative proposal about expanding their dream pastry distribution network through Vistani connections. [[Morgantha]] was intrigued enough to summon her daughters [[Bella Sunbane]] and [[Offalia Wormwiggle]] downstairs, leaving the upper floors unguarded. While all three hags discussed the business opportunity, the invisible team infiltrated the building. Following [[Franz]]'s dying words ("*The gnarled arm holds the key*"), [[Fig Fleetfoot]] discovered a copper key hidden in the millstone's gnarled wooden crossbeam. [[Choppy Gibbles]] used it to open a secret compartment containing the hags' coven contract (written on dried human skin, revealing their true names: Morgantha Stormreaver, Belladonna Sunbane, and Offalia Wormwiggle), along with cheap jewelry and a *charm of mass heroism*. The team then rescued Fyodor and Myrtle from the third floor bedroom. [[Fig Fleetfoot]] used [[Varnish Gothorn]]'s *Tale of the Runaway* to teleport the children and [[Choppy Gibbles]] to safety outside, then signaled with a mighty "*caw-CAW!*" [[Arden Nalero]] feigned receiving a catastrophic call on a fake "speaking stone" about their company being bought out by private equity, allowing the businessmen to withdraw their offer and exit. The hags remained completely unaware that their contract had been stolen and the children rescued. [[Lady Fiona Wachter]] now possesses the true names needed to create a binding circle to trap the night hags for the coming battle.
