# Overview

An ancient stone tower built by the archmage [[Khazan]], located at the end of a gravel causeway extending into [[Lake Baratok]]. The tower is protected by powerful enchantments and magical guardians. In recent times, it has served as a hideout for Dr. Rudolph van Richten (disguised as [[Rictavio]]).

# What the party knows

From [[Session 17 - The Tower and The Truth|Session 17]], the party learned:

## The Tower's Defenses

- The tower door bears a massive wax-like seal with eight buttons representing the eight schools of magic
- The buttons only appear when someone approaches within 5 feet of the door
- Touching the door without solving the puzzle delivers an electric shock
- Attempting to force the door triggers a lightning bolt that extends 100 feet along the causeway
- The door's puzzle can be solved using [[Khazan]]'s [[DM Wiki/Entities/Items/Signet Ring|signet ring]], which shows the proper sequence: evocation, divination, illusion, transmutation, conjuration, abjuration, enchantment, necromancy
- Four stone gargoyles (disguised as griffon statues) guard the roof and animate when the door is attacked or intruders use the exterior scaffolding

## Inside the Tower

The party discovered several floors connected by a golem-powered elevator:

**Third Floor:**
- An old, rotted crib (the floor collapsed when [[Fig Fleetfoot|Fig]] investigated it)

**Fourth Floor:**
- A [[Burned Journal Page]] bearing Dr. Rudolph van Richten's handwriting
- A singed sketch of a sign proclaiming "[[Rictavio]]'s Carnival of Wonders"
- [[Arabelle]]'s beaded bracelet wrapped around a silvered dagger and the [[21-022.hooded-one.png|Hooded One]] Tarokka card (deliberately left as clues)
- A functional suit of plate armor (claimed by [[Arden Nalero|Arden]])
- A wood stove containing the burned documents

**In the Lake:**
- Empty ceramic paint pots (bright yellow and white) sunk beneath lily pads near the shore
- The yellow paint matched the color on [[Rictavio]]'s carnival wagon

## The Tower's Purpose

- Originally built by the archmage [[Khazan]] as a personal refuge
- Contains a permanent antimagic field that prevents scrying
- Recently used by Dr. Rudolph van Richten as a hideout while planning his campaign against [[Strahd von Zarovich|Strahd]]
- Van Richten painted his wagon here, transforming it into "Rictavio's Carnival of Wonders" to hide in plain sight

# Notable places

- **Tower Door** - Arcane puzzle lock requiring knowledge of the eight schools of magic
- **Tower Roof** - Four gargoyle guardians disguised as griffon statues
- **Third Floor** - Contains remnants of domestic life (the crib)
- **Fourth Floor** - Van Richten's former living quarters and work space
- **The Causeway** - Gravel path extending into Lake Baratok, vulnerable to the door's lightning trap

# Notable figures

- [[Khazan]] - The archmage who originally built the tower
- Dr. Rudolph van Richten (disguised as [[Rictavio]]) - Recently used the tower as a hideout
- [[Arabelle]] - Was brought here by Van Richten after her rescue from [[Yan]] and [[Escher]]

# Visits

**[[Session 17 - The Tower and The Truth]]** (Neyavr 6) - The party arrived with [[Szoldar Szoldarovich]] and [[Yevgeni Krushkin]] as guides. After [[Varnish Gothorn|Varnish]] received a mild shock from touching the door and [[Fig Fleetfoot|Fig]]'s flying kick triggered the lightning trap, four gargoyles animated and attacked. The battle was harrowing—gargoyles grappled and dropped [[Varnish Gothorn|Varnish]] and [[Arden Nalero|Arden]]—but the party adapted using [[Choppy Gibbles|Choppy]]'s thunder magic (which the gargoyles were vulnerable to), Arden's *Magic Weapon* spell, and Varnish's *Hold Person*. After defeating the guardians, [[Choppy Gibbles|Choppy]] used the [[DM Wiki/Entities/Items/Signet Ring|platinum signet ring]]'s pattern to solve the door puzzle and gain entry. Inside, they discovered evidence connecting [[Rictavio]] to [[Arabelle]]'s disappearance: the journal page, carnival sign sketch, Arabelle's bracelet and Tarokka card, and paint pots in the lake. [[Varnish Gothorn|Varnish]]'s bed-jumping antics caused the fourth-floor bed to collapse through all floors to the ground level, and [[Fig Fleetfoot|Fig]] fell through the rotted third floor while investigating the crib. The party also found and claimed a suit of plate armor. This evidence led directly to the confrontation with Rictavio at the [[Arasek Stockyard]].
