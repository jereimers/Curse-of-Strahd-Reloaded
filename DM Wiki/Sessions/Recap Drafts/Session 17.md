# Probing Khazan's Tower
- %%CLAUDE: reference for this section is at [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#E6b. The Tower]] %%
- This includes the investigation of the front door as well as the fight with the gargoyles
- Varnish was the first to approach the door, touching it and receiving a mild electric shock
- Then Fig did a flying jump kick to attempt to break the door down, but the door did not budge. Instead, a bolt of lightning erupted from the door, damaging Varnish and Fig and barely missing Choppy and Arden. The bolt was long enough that it reached the other end of the causeway, sputtering out at the feet of Szoldar and Yevgeni, who waited on the lakeshore.
- The lightning awoke the gargoyles, who animated and descended from their perches to attack the party. The gargoyles were named for the characters from the '90s-era cartoon of the same name (Goliath, Hudson, Lexington, and Brooklyn) and each picked a party member to focus on. The early rounds of the battle were harrowing for the players, as they struggled to deal effective damage (Fig's katana glanced off Lexington, dealing only half damage) while the gargoyles grappled both Varnish and Arden, flying them 10 feet up before biting them and then dropping them. The party figured it out eventually, though: Choppy used his Chromatic Orb to do double-effective thunder damage, Arden used Magic Weapon to negate the gargoyles' resistance to his slashing attacks, and Varnish used a third-level Hold Person spell to neutralize Hudson and Goliath. From there, the battle wrapped quickly
- After dispatching the gargoyles, the party turned to the tower's wooden door. Varnish, Fig, and Choppy recognized the symbols that appeared on the door's large wax-like seal as corresponding to the eight schools of magic (as described in [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#1. The Tower Door]]), and Choppy brought out the [[DM Wiki/Entities/Items/Signet Ring|Signet Ring]] to solve the door's puzzle lock.

# Investigating the Tower 
- %%CLAUDE: reference for this section is at [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#3. Inside the Tower]] %%
- Choppy, Arden, and Varnish took the golem-powered elevator.
- Fig went up the scaffolding and met the other three party members on the third floor.
- Fig investigated the baby's crib on the third level, causing the floor to collapse and taking some falling damage.
- The party investigated the top level thoroughly: Varnish started jumping on the bed, causing it to fall all the way to the ground level (but not taking damage, though he did have to clamber all the way back up), while Fig investigated the wood stove and discovered the [[Burned Journal Page]] and the first draft of the sign for "Rictavio's Carnival of Wonders" ([[rictavio's-carnival-sign.png]]). Meanwhile Arden investigated the suit of armor, finding it fit him well enough to take it and wear it, increasing his AC to 18. Varnish found the bracelet, dagger, and tarokka card (the Hooded One), using his knowledge of the occult as a [[College of Spirits (VRGR)]] Bard to interpret the meaning of the card for the party: mystery, newcomers, and hidden identities. 
- Fig looked out the window and noticed the yellow-stained lilypads. When the party left the tower, they dove down to the bottom of the lake and dredged up the empty paint pots that "Rictavio" had used to paint his wagon just a few days prior. After this, the party began the one-hour trek back to Vallaki. They would confront Rictavio, whom they now knew to be intimately connected to both Arabelle and this "Dr. Rudolph van Richten" character

# Another Run-in with Werewolves
- %%CLAUDE: reference for this section is at [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#E7. The Road Back]] %%
- The party encountered Zsolt and Mathilda as described in the source material. Zsolt warned the players about the "wandering Vistana," but the party didn't take his warning seriously. Arden, ever suspicious, moved to interrogate Zsolt by placing his silver battleaxe against his neck shortly after Szoldar indicated that he knew not these particular "trappers". 
- Before Zsolt and Mathilda could say much more, they'd both transformed into their hybrid forms and attacked the players. 
- The werewolves proved little match for the prepared party—Zsolt quickly fell to Arden's silvered axe, at which point Mathilda attempted to flee but was taken out by some well-placed silver arrows from Szoldar and Yevgeni. 
- The party pulled their final punches as they finished off Mathilda, allowing Varnish to cast *Suggestion* and learn some valuable intel about the werewolves of the Svalich Woods:
	- The den is located on the northwestern shore of Lake Baratok
	- The pack is being led by Kiril Stoyanovich
	- The pack was not involved in [[Arabelle]]'s kidnapping, and she is not being held at their den.
- Varnish finished things for Mathilda by casting *Mind Sliver*, ending her life there by the side of the Old Svalich Road

# Confronting Rictavio
- %%CLAUDE: reference for this section is at [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#E8c. Rictavio ’s Wagon]] %%
- After parting with the hunters, the party decided to go directly to the Arasek Stockyard to catch Rictavio at his wagon. 
- Varnish noticed that Rictavio was repetitiously pretending to repair a spoke on his wagon wheel while apparently speaking with someone. Varnish confronted the ringmaster directly: "Whatcha doin' there?" and refusing to accept his cover story (that he was "fixing the wagon wheel").
- Arden, full of righteous anger at the prospect that Rictavio had abducted (and was potentially endangering) Arabelle, pulled his silver-battleaxe-to-the-throat move once again and demanded that Rictavio come clean.
- Rictavio parried Arden's advances with his own cane-sword, sidestepping the dark elf's blade and demanding to know that the players were not servants of Strahd before revealing anything further. 
- Choppy revealed the [[DM Wiki/Entities/Items/Signet Ring|Signet Ring]], Varnish the dagger and bracelet and tarokka card, and Fig the [[Burned Journal Page]] and scrap of cloth embroidered "R.V.R." — they had him dead to rights, and demanded that he come clean. 
- From within the wagon, a small voice (that of Arabelle) was heard saying, "They mean me no harm, Mister Doctor sir." At that, Rictavio doffed his *hat of disguise*, transforming his visage and demeanor from that of a flamboyant carnival barker to a weathered, wizened monster hunter. "Very well, Fraulein Arabelle. My name, masters, is Dr. Rudolph van Richten. It is a pleasure to make your acquaintance," he said with a Germanic accent as he opened the door to his wagon, revealing a young Vistana child clutching a stuffed sabertooth tiger, "and this is Arabelle."
