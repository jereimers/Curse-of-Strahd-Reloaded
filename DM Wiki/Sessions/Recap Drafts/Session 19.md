# Morning Muster
- First, before anything else happened, Arden soiled his pants while trancing overnight due to an after-effect of Rahadin's psychic screams. He tossed his shitty skivvies out the window of the Blue Water Inn, where they landed atop a fencepost. Nobody else was awake.
- Varnish conducted his daily séance using his Tarokka deck gifted from Lady Wachter. He channeled the story of the Runaway, which would come in extremely handy later. %%CLAUDE: see [[College of Spirits (VRGR)]] for this tale's content%%
- Then the party started scheming for the day's activities: visiting Old Bonegrinder to rescue the Barovian children sold to the hags by their father, Fyodor and Myrtle, and to retrieve the hags' true names for Lady Wachter's use in creating a binding circle so that they could be effectively fought.
- They retrieved Victor, who uncharacteristically met them at his house's front door and was eager to accompany them. Patches, his skeletal cat, took a liking to Fig and hopped on the harengon's head while Arden scratched the inside of the skeletal cat's ear
- The party then retrieved Ireena from Father Lucien's church, finding her talking to some congregants in the aftermath of their morning service. Ireena was thrilled to accompany them. 
- Finally, they stopped by Wachterhaus to pick up Lady Wachter. Upon arrival, they were informed by Haliq that Lady Wachter foresaw their mission today in a Tarokka reading and already awaited them at the Morning Gate, along with her four associates Boris, Andrej, Miruna, and Ruxandra.

# Old Bonegrinder
- %%CLAUDE: the bulk of the session's activity took place here. The guiding source material is [[Arc H - The Lost Soul#H7a. Raiding the Windmill]]%%
- The party paused to discuss strategy with Victor and Lady Wachter. Fiona's familiar, Majesto the imp, conducted preliminary recon while Lady Wachter relayed his observations via their psychic link. %%CLAUDE: Majesto described each floor of the mill, as described in [[06_Chapter 6 - Old Bonegrinder#Areas of the Windmill]].%%
- They then recruited Victor's help in the form of a couple of Greater Invisibility spells, allowing Fig and Choppy to become the invisible infiltration team while Arden and Varnish served as the diversion team. Varnish used "disguise self" to give himself a charismatic edge on his deception checks. Varnish became "Emric Cantemir" and Arden became "Radu Tyminski", posing as real estate entrepreneurs in preparation for their chat with the shrewd Morgantha. Critically, Varnish imparted his Tale of the Runaway to Fig, enabling them to extricate themselves and up to 6 others via silent *Misty Step*
- The operation then began: Fig and Choppy invisibly sidled up to the windmill's door. Then Varnish and Arden, posing as "Tyrone & Sons Holdings, LLC", announced loudly from the doorstep: “Good evening, esteemed proprietors—Radu Tyminski acquisitions and enforcement. And this distinguished gentleman beside me is Emric Cantemir, here to discuss… asset redistribution. We’ve brought an opportunity that could double your yield… but only if discussed face-to-face.”
- Morgantha opened the door, evaluated the pair of businessmen, and bought their ruse enough to invite them inside for further discussion. Meanwhile, Choppy and Fig snuck inside through the open door and made their way up the stairs.
- Varnish and Arden made a convincing three-fold pitch to the hag: they would leverage their connections among the Vistani to 1) market pastries to additional children, thus 2) bringing a higher volume of children to the windmill, and 3) eventually expanding the dream pastry distribution network beyond the Valley of Barovia (since the Vistani could traverse the mists at will). This offer was enticing enough that Morgantha invited her daughters downstairs to confer. The daughters, Bella and Offalia, narrowly missed tripping over Choppy and Fig on the stairway but made it downstairs without detecting the invisible duo, joining their mother excitedly.
- Once the upper floors were deserted of hags, Choppy and Fig got to snooping. Remembering Franz's portentous phrasing (*"The gnarled arm holds the key"*), Fig investigated the millstone and found that one of its wooden crossbeams was, indeed, more gnarled than the others. Fig pulled on it and found it detachable, revealing upon its removal a narrow slot into which was jammed a copper key. 
- Fig handed the key to Choppy, who used it to open the millstone's secret side compartment. Inside they found a small collection of cheap jewelry, a *charm of mass heroism*, and the hags' coven contract written on dried human skin. Shuddering, reading it, shuddering again, then tucking it away, Choppy and Fig ascended to the bedroom.
- Inside they found Fyodor and Myrtle huddled in stacked crates. Choppy and Fig spoke to them from their invisible positions at first, startling the children and forcing a final check for stealthiness. They revealed themselves to the children (erm... dropped their invisibility) and calmed them down by saying they were here to rescue them and they wouldn't be hurt any more. Choppy, in his unhinged kind of way, urged them to be silent by instructing them to "never speak out loud again," prompting a fit of quickly stifled nervous laughter from the kids. Fig then used the teleportation ability granted by Varnish's *Tale of the Runaway*  to exfiltrate Choppy, Fyodor, Myrtle, and themselves. Once safely outside, Fig let loose a mighty "*caw-CAW!*" to signal to "Tyrone & Sons" that it was time to skedaddle.
- "Radu" pretended to get a phone call on his fake *speaking stone* (eliciting an impressed comment uttered by Morgantha about how legitimate their business must be). Answering, he pantomimed his side of a catastrophic business conversation: their operation had been bought out and their assets were being liquidated. "Island?... Killed himself??... Ranch?... Overdosed?!" he was heard to say, while Varnish spat out a curse, "fucking private equity!!!". Arden "hung up" his "speaking stone" and informed the hags that they would need to withdraw their offer immediately while they evaluated the impact of their impending liquidation. Disappointed but unsuspicious, Morgantha allowed them to leave the building. Outside, they reconvened with their party and prepared to escort Fyodor and Myrtle back to the Barovian Refugee Camp (now located in the Vallakian town center) before eventually returning to Old Bonegrinder to put a permanent stop to the hags' *business dealings*. %%CLAUDE: the session ended immediately after Varnish and Arden left the windmill. The party's intentions were obvious, however, and the next session will certainly involve combat with the hags.%%
