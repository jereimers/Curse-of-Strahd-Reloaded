# Mediating the Case of Arrigal v. van Richten
- %%CLAUDE: reference for this section is at [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#E8c. Rictavio ’s Wagon]] %%
- Picking up from the end of last session ([[Session 17 - The Tower and The Truth#Confronting Rictavio]]), the party heard Rudolph's recount of the events leading to the intertwining of his fate's with the young Vistana, [[Arabelle]] %%CLAUDE: as detailed in [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#E8a. Confronting Rictavio]] and [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#Meeting Arabelle]]%%
- When Arrigal appeared, the party intervened on Arabelle's behalf to broker an agreement between the Vistana and van Richten.
- They learned that Arrigal has been working with Strahd, but was truthfully unaware of Strahd's bounty on children matching Arabelle's description and would not have allowed the plan to succeed if he'd known about it %%CLAUDE: as detailed in [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#Arrigal 's Arrival]] and [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#Brokering an Agreement]]%%
- The party ultimately brokered an agreement where van Richten would move his wagon to the Vallaki's nearby Vistani camp, close to her family, and Arrigal would feed Strahd false information about Arabelle's whereabouts.
- Arabelle then delivered her prophecy %%CLAUDE: as detailed in [[Arc E - The Missing Vistana#Arabelle 's Prophecy]]%%
- The party accompanied Arrigal, Rudolph, and Arabelle to the Vistani camp. En route, Rudolph told them about his arrival in Barovia and experiences with Doru and Escher. Reaching the encampment, Luvash tearfully embraced his daughter and promised that the Vallaki Vistani were in the party's debt.

# An Invitation from the Devil
- Rahadin caught the party leaving Wachterhaus, having been let in by the town guards at the Morning Gate. 
- He delivered [[Strahd's Invitation]] %%CLAUDE: as detailed in [[Arc O - Dinner with the Devil#O1. The Invitation]]%%. The party tried real hard to "big dog" the dusk elf chamberlain. They defaced the invitation by burning it, then urinating on it, then stomping on it before Choppy created a little dirt homunculus to urinate on the invitation once more. Despite their blatant disrespect, the party RSVP'd "yes" and said they'd be there, and Rahadin turned up the volume on his ethereal psychic soul shrieking and left. 

# Law & Order: Vallaki
- Remember how [[Milivoj]] and [[Henrik van der Voort]] conspired to steal [[The Bones of St. Andral]]? Rahadin remembered, and he decided he'd make two deliveries to Vallaki this evening: one was the Count's invitation, the other was the Count's justice. 
- %%CLAUDE: as detailed in [[Arc D - St. Andral's Feast#D6b. The Trial]]%% Yeska met the party, begging them to come to the town square to intercede on Milivoj's and Henrik's behalf. 
- The party arrived to find the two men shackled and kneeling next to Rahadin; Milivoj's distraught siblings wailing in the crowd, while Father Lucian implored Rahadin to see reason. 
- Our heroes engaged Rahadin, arguing that both men had acted under the coercion from Volenta Popofsky and that they were otherwise good-natured individuals with no criminal histories who didn't deserve to be severely punished. Through persuasion, they succeeded in mitigating their sentences to moderate: both men received 12 lashes, which they bore with dignity. 
- Afterward, the party stepped in to heal the flogged men (Arden with his "lay on hands", Varnish with his "healing word") before departing to the Blue Water Inn for the evening.
